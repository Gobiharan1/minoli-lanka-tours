Day-by-Day Itinerary
Day 1: Airport Arrival → Pinnawala → Kandy Upon arrival at Bandaranaike International Airport, meet your English-speaking driver-guide from Minoli Lanka Tours. Depending on your flight schedule, visit the renowned Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage to witness the feeding and bathing of rescued elephants. Continue to Kandy, Sri Lanka’s cultural capital. Check into your hotel and relax. Overnight: Kandy Hotel
Day 2: Kandy → Sigiriya Area → Minneriya Safari → Kandy Early morning drive to Sigiriya. Climb the iconic Sigiriya Rock Fortress (or opt for the less crowded Pidurangala Rock for stunning views). Enjoy a relaxing Sigiriya village tour to experience local rural life. On the return journey, visit Nalanda Gedige, Dambulla Cave Temple (ancient rock caves with Buddha statues), Matale Hindu Temple, and a spice garden (with interactive tour and tasting). End the day with an exciting evening Minneriya Safari to witness wild elephants and other wildlife. Overnight: Kandy Hotel
Day 3: Kandy Sightseeing → Nuwara Eliya → Kandy Morning visits in Kandy: Temple of the Tooth Relic, Kandy View Point, and Bahirawakanda Buddha Statue. Proceed to the beautiful Peradeniya Botanical Gardens. Drive uphill via Ramboda Falls to Nuwara Eliya. Explore a tea factory and estate, the historic Post Office, and scenic Gregory Lake. Enjoy the cool climate and colonial atmosphere before returning to Kandy. Overnight: Kandy Hotel
Day 4: Kandy → Pinnawala (Optional) → Airport Departure Depending on your flight time, enjoy a relaxed morning. If you missed it on Day 1, revisit Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage. Your driver will transfer you safely to the airport for your departure flight, marking the end of your memorable Sri Lanka tour with Minoli Lanka Tours.
