Your Kandy City Tour Highlights
You’ll visit Kandy’s iconic sites with comfortable pacing and meaningful stories:
	•	Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic (Sri Dalada Maligawa): A UNESCO World Heritage site housing the sacred tooth of Lord Buddha. You’ll witness its spiritual importance and rich history in a respectful atmosphere.
	•	Kandy Lake: Stroll around this beautiful artificial lake surrounded by lush scenery and colonial charm — perfect for peaceful photos and reflection.
	•	Bahirawakanda Big Buddha: Climb to the hilltop for stunning panoramic views of the city and surrounding hills.
	•	Royal Botanical Gardens, Peradeniya: Wander through one of Asia’s finest gardens, home to thousands of plant species, vibrant orchids, and majestic trees.
	•	Kandy View Point: Capture breathtaking vistas of the lake and city below.
	•	Ceylon Tea & Craft Experiences: Visit a tea factory for a guided tour and tasting, plus stops at wood carving or batik workshops to see local artisans at work.
Definite Highlight: You will enjoy a vibrant Kandyan Cultural Dance Show — a colorful evening performance featuring traditional drumming, graceful dances, and fiery spectacles that capture the essence of Sri Lankan heritage.
