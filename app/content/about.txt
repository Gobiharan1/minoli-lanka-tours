Our Story (Left image of Kandy scenery or team; right text)
Minoli Lanka Tours was born in the cultural heart of Sri Lanka — Kandy. As a passionate, locally rooted inbound tour operator, we understand what travelers truly seek: not just sightseeing, but meaningful connections, comfort, safety, and the freedom to explore at their own pace.
With years of hands-on experience in Sri Lanka’s tourism industry, we started Minoli Lanka Tours to offer something different — private tours delivered with the same care and attention we would give to our own family. Today, we proudly help travelers from around the world create unforgettable memories across this beautiful island.

Who We Are
We are a dedicated team of local travel professionals based in Kandy. Our English-speaking driver-guides are not just experts on routes and attractions — they are warm, attentive hosts who go above and beyond to ensure you feel safe, supported, and genuinely cared for throughout your journey.
We operate a well-maintained fleet of comfortable, air-conditioned private vehicles and work only with trusted partners for accommodations, activities, and services. Every detail is handled with professionalism, transparency, and a personal touch that sets us apart.

Our Vision
