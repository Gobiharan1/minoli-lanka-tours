Day-by-Day Itinerary
Day 1: Airport Arrival → Negombo Meet your Minoli Lanka Tours driver-guide at Bandaranaike International Airport. Short transfer to Negombo for beach relaxation and jet-lag recovery. Overnight: Negombo Hotel
Day 2: Negombo → Anuradhapura → Sigiriya Drive to Anuradhapura and explore the ancient sacred sites and Atamasthana (eight sacred places). Continue to Sigiriya. Overnight: Sigiriya Hotel
Day 3: Polonnaruwa Ancient City & Minneriya Safari Full day exploring Polonnaruwa ancient city (UNESCO) with its impressive ruins. Enjoy a traditional Sri Lankan lunch. Evening Minneriya National Park wildlife safari. Overnight: Sigiriya Hotel
Day 4: Sigiriya → Kandy Morning climb of Sigiriya Rock Fortress (or Pidurangala). Enjoy a Sigiriya village tour. On the way to Kandy, visit Dambulla Cave Temple, Nalanda Gedige, a spice garden in Matale, and Matale Hindu Temple. Overnight: Kandy Hotel
Day 5: Kandy City Tour Explore Temple of the Tooth Relic, Kandy View Point, Bahirawakanda Temple, and Peradeniya Botanical Gardens. Evening cultural dance show. Overnight: Kandy Hotel
Day 6: Kandy → Ella Drive via Ramboda Falls, a tea estate and factory, Nuwara Eliya Post Office, and Gregory Lake. Take the scenic train ride from Nanu Oya to Ella. Overnight: Ella Hotel
Day 7: Ella Exploration Visit the iconic Nine Arch Bridge and hike to Little Adam’s Peak. Enjoy Flying Ravana adventure (zipline) and relax in Ella. Overnight: Ella Hotel
Day 8: Ella → Tissamaharama Morning visit to Ravana Falls. Afternoon/evening Yala National Park safari. Overnight: Tissamaharama Hotel
Day 9: Tissamaharama → Mirissa Drive to Mirissa with stops at Coconut Tree Hill and Hummanaya Blowhole. Relax on Mirissa Beach. Overnight: Mirissa Hotel
Day 10: Mirissa Beach Day Early morning whale watching tour. Free time to relax on Mirissa Beach. Overnight: Mirissa Hotel
Day 11: Mirissa → Unawatuna Visit stilt fishermen and enjoy a Koggala Lake boat ride. Relax on Dalawella or Unawatuna beaches. Overnight: Unawatuna Hotel
Day 12: Unawatuna → Bentota Explore Rumassala Temple and Jungle Beach. Visit Galle Fort and a turtle hatchery. Relax on Bentota Beach. Overnight: Bentota Hotel
Day 13: Bentota → Colombo Morning Madu River boat safari. Transfer to Colombo for a Colombo city tour (markets, landmarks, and beachfront). Overnight: Colombo Hotel
Day 14: Colombo → Airport Morning at leisure (if time permits, more Colombo sightseeing). Safe private transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport for your departure. Tour Ends
