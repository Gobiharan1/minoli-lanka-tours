Day-by-Day Itinerary
Day 1: Airport Arrival → Sigiriya Meet your Minoli Lanka Tours driver-guide at Bandaranaike International Airport. Scenic transfer to Sigiriya and check into your hotel. Relax and recover from your journey. Overnight: Sigiriya Hotel
Day 2: Polonnaruwa Ancient City & Minneriya Safari Drive to Polonnaruwa and explore the magnificent ancient city (UNESCO site) with its well-preserved ruins, statues, and monuments. Enjoy a traditional Sri Lankan lunch. Evening Minneriya National Park wildlife safari to see wild elephants and other animals. Overnight: Sigiriya Hotel
Day 3: Sigiriya → Kandy Morning climb of Sigiriya Rock Fortress (or Pidurangala). Enjoy a Sigiriya village tour. On the way to Kandy, visit Dambulla Cave Temple, Nalanda Gedige, a spice garden in Matale, and Matale Hindu Temple. Overnight: Kandy Hotel
Day 4: Kandy City Tour Full day exploring Kandy: Temple of the Tooth Relic, Kandy View Point, Bahirawakanda Temple, and Peradeniya Botanical Gardens. Evening cultural dance show. Overnight: Kandy Hotel
Day 5: Kandy → Ella Drive through the highlands with stops at Ramboda Falls, a tea estate and factory, Nuwara Eliya Post Office, and Gregory Lake. Take the scenic train ride from Nanu Oya to Ella. Overnight: Ella Hotel
Day 6: Ella Exploration Visit the iconic Nine Arch Bridge and hike to Little Adam’s Peak. Relax and enjoy the cool mountain atmosphere of Ella. Overnight: Ella Hotel
Day 7: Ella → Mirissa Morning visit to Ravana Falls. Afternoon Yala National Park safari. Continue to Mirissa. Overnight: Mirissa Hotel
Day 8: Mirissa Beach Day Early morning whale watching tour. Relax on Mirissa Beach and visit Coconut Tree Hill for panoramic views. Free time to unwind. Overnight: Mirissa Hotel
Day 9: Mirissa → Bentota Visit Galle Fort (UNESCO site). Stop at a turtle hatchery and enjoy a Madu River boat safari. Relax on Bentota Beach. Overnight: Bentota Hotel
Day 10: Bentota → Colombo (Optional) → Airport Morning at leisure on Bentota Beach. If flight time permits, enjoy a short Colombo city tour. Safe transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport for your departure. Tour Ends
