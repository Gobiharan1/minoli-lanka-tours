Day-by-Day Itinerary
Day 1: Airport Arrival → Sigiriya Meet your Minoli Lanka Tours driver-guide at Bandaranaike International Airport (Katunayake). Enjoy a scenic drive to Sigiriya through lush countryside. Settle into your hotel and relax after your journey. Overnight: Sigiriya Hotel
Day 2: Sigiriya Exploration & Minneriya Safari Morning climb of Sigiriya Rock Fortress (or the alternative Pidurangala Rock for breathtaking views). Follow with a peaceful Sigiriya village tour to experience authentic rural Sri Lankan life. In the evening, enjoy an exciting wildlife safari at Minneriya National Park, famous for its large elephant gatherings. Overnight: Sigiriya Hotel
Day 3: Sigiriya → Kandy Morning visits to Dambulla Cave Temple, Nalanda Gedige, Matale Hindu Temple, and a spice garden (interactive tour with tasting). Drive to Kandy and visit the Temple of the Tooth Relic, Kandy View Point, and Bahirawakanda Buddha Statue. End the day with a colorful cultural dance show. Overnight: Kandy Hotel
Day 4: Kandy → Nuwara Eliya Visit the beautiful Peradeniya Botanical Gardens. Drive through the highlands, stopping at Ramboda Falls. Explore a tea estate and factory (with tasting), the historic Nuwara Eliya Post Office, and scenic Gregory Lake. Enjoy the cool climate of “Little England.” Overnight: Nuwara Eliya Hotel
Day 5: Nuwara Eliya → Ella Take the famous scenic train ride from Nanu Oya to Ella — one of the most beautiful railway journeys in the world. In Ella, visit the iconic Nine Arch Bridge and hike to Little Adam’s Peak for panoramic views. Overnight: Ella Hotel
Day 6: Ella → Mirissa Morning visit to Ravana Falls. Continue south for an afternoon Yala National Park safari (leopards, elephants, and birdlife). Relax at Mirissa Beach and enjoy sunset views at Coconut Tree Hill. Overnight: Mirissa Hotel
Day 7: Mirissa → Galle (Optional) → Airport Depending on your flight schedule, visit the historic Galle Fort (UNESCO site with colonial charm). Otherwise, enjoy some beach time before your private transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport for departure. Tour Ends
