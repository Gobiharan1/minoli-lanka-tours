Welcome to Your Nuwara Eliya Day Tour
Leave the warmth of Kandy behind and climb into Sri Lanka’s refreshing highlands on a memorable private day tour to Nuwara Eliya with Minoli Lanka Tours. Known as "Little England," this colonial-era hill station offers cool breezes, rolling tea estates, cascading waterfalls, and a relaxed, elegant atmosphere that feels worlds away.
As your Kandy-based team, we craft a comfortable, personalized journey with safe driving, flexible pacing, and warm, attentive service. You’ll feel genuinely cared for — with honest guidance, quick adaptations to weather or your energy, and the kind of thoughtful touches that make solo travelers, couples, and small groups love traveling with us. This isn’t just a day trip; it’s a refreshing highland escape tailored to you.

Tour Highlights
	•	Ramboda Falls & Viewpoint — Marvel at one of Sri Lanka’s tallest waterfalls and enjoy scenic mountain vistas.
	•	Tea Plantation & Factory — Walk through lush green estates and learn the full Ceylon tea-making process with a fresh tasting.
	•	Nuwara Eliya Town ("Little England") — Stroll colonial-era streets, visit the historic Post Office, and feel the old-world charm.
	•	Gregory Lake — Relax by the serene lake, enjoy boat rides (optional), or simply take in the peaceful surroundings.
	•	Hakgala Botanical Gardens (optional) — Explore beautiful gardens with roses, ferns, and monkey-filled landscapes.
	•	Seetha Amman Temple (optional) — A scenic Ramayana-linked temple in a tranquil setting.
.
