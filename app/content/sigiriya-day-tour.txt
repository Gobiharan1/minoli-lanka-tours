Tour Highlights
	•	Matale Hindu Temple — Admire vibrant architecture and spiritual artistry.
	•	Spice Garden — Engage your senses with a guided walk through aromatic herbs and spices, plus optional Ayurvedic insights.
	•	Dambulla Cave Temple — Explore this UNESCO-listed marvel with ancient Buddha statues and stunning cave murals.
	•	Sigiriya Rock Fortress (or Pidurangala alternative) — Climb the iconic Lion Rock for panoramic views and fascinating history (or choose the serene Pidurangala hike).
	•	Village Experience (optional) — Get a taste of rural Sri Lankan life.
	•	Wildlife Safari (Minneriya / Kaudulla / Hurulu) — End with an exciting jeep safari to see elephants and other wildlife in their natural habitat.
