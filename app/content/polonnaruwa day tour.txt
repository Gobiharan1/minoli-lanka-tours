Tour Highlights
	•	Polonnaruwa Ancient City — Wander through the UNESCO-listed royal palace complex, audience halls, and sacred quadrangle.
	•	Gal Vihara — Marvel at the iconic rock-cut Buddha statues, including the impressive reclining Buddha.
	•	Rankot Vihara & Other Dagobas — Admire towering stupas and monastic ruins.
	•	Shiva Temple & Lotus Pond — Discover Hindu influences and beautiful ancient engineering.
	•	Scenic Reservoir Views — Enjoy peaceful stops by the ancient man-made lakes.
	•	Optional Add-ons — Minneriya safari for elephants (if time and interest allow) or a village lunch experience.
