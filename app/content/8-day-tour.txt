Discover Sri Lanka’s Beaches, Wildlife, Hill Country & Ancient Wonders This private 8-day tour with Minoli Lanka Tours offers the perfect mix of relaxation on golden beaches, thrilling wildlife safaris, misty highlands, scenic train rides, and rich cultural heritage. Day-by-Day Itinerary
Day 1: Airport Arrival → Bentota Meet your Minoli Lanka Tours driver-guide at Bandaranaike International Airport. Transfer to Bentota on the beautiful southern coast. Relax at your beach hotel and unwind after your journey. Overnight: Bentota Hotel
Day 2: Bentota → Mirissa Enjoy a scenic Madu River boat ride through mangroves. Visit a turtle hatchery to learn about conservation. Explore the historic Galle Fort (UNESCO site) and watch traditional stilt fishermen. Continue to Mirissa. Overnight: Mirissa Hotel
Day 3: Mirissa Beach Day Early morning whale watching tour (one of the best spots in the world). Relax on Mirissa Beach, visit Coconut Tree Hill for stunning views, and enjoy free time by the sea. Overnight: Mirissa Hotel
Day 4: Mirissa → Ella Morning drive to Yala for an exciting Yala National Park safari (leopards, elephants, and birds). Visit Ravana Falls en route to Ella. Overnight: Ella Hotel
Day 5: Ella → Nuwara Eliya Morning visit to the iconic Nine Arch Bridge and short hike to Little Adam’s Peak. Take the famous scenic train ride from Ella to Nanu Oya. In Nuwara Eliya, explore the historic Post Office and Gregory Lake. Overnight: Nuwara Eliya Hotel
Day 6: Nuwara Eliya → Kandy Drive via Ramboda Falls and a tea estate & factory visit. Explore Peradeniya Botanical Gardens. In Kandy, enjoy an evening cultural dance show and visit the Temple of the Tooth Relic, Kandy View Point, and Bahirawakanda Temple. Overnight: Kandy Hotel
Day 7: Kandy → Sigiriya Morning stops at Matale Hindu Temple and a spice garden. Visit Dambulla Cave Temple. In Sigiriya, enjoy a village tour, climb Sigiriya Rock Fortress (or Pidurangala), and finish with an evening Minneriya wildlife safari. Overnight: Sigiriya Hotel
Day 8: Sigiriya → Airport Morning at leisure (depending on flight time). Your driver will transfer you safely to Bandaranaike International Airport to conclude your memorable Sri Lanka tour. Tour Ends
